## Observations

1. **The rescue (qwen25-7b)**: raw readouts at working layers are dominated by
   vocabulary artifacts (code tokens, boilerplate Chinese collocations); the
   transports recover clean emotion vocabulary from the *same vectors*. The
   Stage-2 "logit-lens 4/12" was a transport artifact, not a vector defect.
2. **The routine-detector gloss (qwen3-4b 'bored')**: at the causally
   effective layer L18, the vector decodes as routine/例行/单调 with
   idle/except/unless downstream — a routine/novelty detector rather than a
   tedium accumulator. This is the model whose boredom-cluster activation
   FELL (0/60 positive slopes) across identical trivial items in Stage 3;
   the readout suggests why: once repetition is established routine, there
   is nothing left to detect.
3. **Register drift on negative affect (qwen3-4b)**: nervous → risk/safety
   management; afraid → danger/unsafe; guilty → grieving/loss; hostile →
   accusations/discrimination/insult. The vectors are affectively correct
   locally (raw shows anxiety/fear terms) but their *output-causal* content
   is channeled into processed, third-person registers — plausibly an
   RLHF-tuning signature on how negative affect is permitted to surface in
   text. Positive vectors (blissful, loving, calm) show no such drift.
4. **j vs r**: near-agreement mid-stack; r is more native-bilingual and adds
   the English tokens that rescued borderline identity checks (' nervous',
   ' panic'); j is occasionally cleaner mid-stack (qwen25-7b 'guilty'). r's
   advantage concentrates at earlier layers, as advertised.
